# Graduate / Master Level - Articles & Papers

Advanced academic papers and research articles for specialized financial knowledge.

---

## 🏆 Foundational Advanced Papers

### 1. **"The Arbitrage Pricing Theory"** (1976)
- **Author**: Stephen A. Ross
- **Journal**: Journal of Economic Theory
- **Topics**: APT, factor models, arbitrage
- **Why Read**: Alternative to CAPM, foundation for factor investing
- **Access**: JSTOR, Google Scholar

### 2. **"Continuous-Time Finance"** Papers by Robert C. Merton
- **Key Paper**: "Theory of Rational Option Pricing" (1973)
- **Journal**: Bell Journal of Economics
- **Why Read**: Nobel Prize work on options pricing
- **Access**: JSTOR

### 3. **"Conditional Heteroskedasticity in Asset Returns"** (1986)
- **Author**: Tim Bollerslev
- **Journal**: Journal of Econometrics
- **Topics**: GARCH models, volatility forecasting
- **Why Read**: Foundation of volatility modeling
- **Access**: ScienceDirect

## 📊 Asset Pricing & Factors

### 4. **"Common Risk Factors in the Returns on Stocks and Bonds"** (1993)
- **Authors**: Fama & French
- **Topics**: Extension of 3-factor to bonds
- **Access**: JSTOR

### 5. **"Value and Momentum Everywhere"** (2013)
- **Authors**: Asness, Moskowitz, Pedersen
- **Journal**: Journal of Finance
- **Topics**: Global factors, value, momentum
- **Why Read**: Shows factors work globally across assets
- **Access**: SSRN, JSTOR

### 6. **"Quality Minus Junk"** (2018)
- **Authors**: Asness, Frazzini, Pedersen
- **Journal**: Review of Accounting Studies
- **Topics**: Quality factor, profitability
- **Why Read**: Defines quality as investable factor
- **Access**: SSRN

## 💹 Options & Derivatives

- **Journal**: Journal of Political Economy
- **Why Read**: Nobel Prize-winning options pricing formula
- **Access**: JSTOR

### 8. **"Volatility is Not Constant"** Papers
- **Authors**: Heston (1993), Hull & White (1987)
- **Topics**: Stochastic volatility models

- Heston, stochastic volatility literature

**Month 4**: Market Microstructure
- Madhavan survey, HFT papers

**Month 5**: Machine Learning
- Gu et al., deep learning survey

**Month 6**: Behavioral & Alternative Data
- Limits to arbitrage, textual analysis
