# Graduate / Master Level - Books

Advanced textbooks and specialized readings for deep financial expertise.

---

## 📊 Asset Pricing & Portfolio Theory

### 1. **"Asset Pricing"** (Revised Edition)
- **Author**: John H. Cochrane
- **Publisher**: Princeton University Press (2005)
- **Level**: Advanced
- **Topics**: Asset pricing theory, factor models, consumption-based models
- **Prerequisites**: Econometrics, calculus, statistics
- **Why Read**: Comprehensive theoretical foundation, PhD-level rigor for master students
- **Price**: ~$60-80
- **Time**: Full semester

### 2. **"Active Portfolio Management"**
- **Authors**: Richard C. Grinold, Ronald N. Kahn
- **Edition**: 2nd Edition (1999)
- **Topics**: Alpha, information ratio, portfolio construction
- **Why Read**: Practitioner guide to quantitative portfolio management
- **Price**: ~$80-120
- **Time**: 6-8 weeks

### 3. **"Quantitative Equity Portfolio Management"**
- **Authors**: Ludwig B. Chincarini, Daehwan Kim
- **Edition**: 2006
- **Topics**: Factor models, risk models, optimization
- **Why Read**: Bridge theory and practice
- **Price**: ~$70-100
- **Time**: 6-8 weeks

## 📈 Options, Derivatives & Risk Management

### 4. **"Options, Futures, and Other Derivatives"** ⭐
- **Author**: John C. Hull
- **Edition**: 11th Edition (2022)
- **Topics**: Options pricing, Greeks, exotic derivatives, risk management
- **Why Read**: Industry standard, comprehensive coverage
- **Price**: ~$150-280 (textbook)
- **Time**: Full semester

### 5. **"Dynamic Hedging"**
- **Author**: Nassim Nicholas Taleb
- **Edition**: 1997
- **Topics**: Options trading, volatility, risk management
- **Why Read**: Practitioner perspective, deep insights into options markets
- **Price**: ~$80-120
- **Time**: 6-8 weeks

### 6. **"The Volatility Surface"**
- **Authors**: Jim Gatheral
- **Edition**: 2006
- **Topics**: Implied volatility, stochastic volatility models
- **Why Read**: Advanced options pricing theory
- **Price**: ~$60-90
- **Time**: 8-10 weeks

## 🧮 Quantitative Finance & Mathematical Finance

### 9. **"Paul Wilmott on Quantitative Finance"** (3-Volume Set)
- **Author**: Paul Wilmott
- **Edition**: 2nd Edition (2006)
- **Topics**: Stochastic calculus, PDE methods, exotic options
- **Why Read**: Comprehensive, practitioner-focused, excellent explanations
- **Price**: ~$150-200 (set)
- **Time**: Reference, full year

### 10. **"Stochastic Calculus for Finance I & II"**
- **Author**: Steven Shreve
- **Edition**: 2004
- **Topics**: Binomial model, continuous-time finance, Brownian motion
- **Why Read**: Rigorous mathematical foundation
- **Prerequisites**: Calculus, probability theory
- **Price**: ~$60-80 each volume
- **Time**: Full semester each

### 11. **"The Concepts and Practice of Mathematical Finance"**
- **Author**: Mark S. Joshi
- **Edition**: 2nd Edition (2008)
- **Topics**: Option pricing, hedging, Monte Carlo methods
- **Why Read**: Bridges theory and implementation
- **Price**: ~$60-80
- **Time**: 8-10 weeks
