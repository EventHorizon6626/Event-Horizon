## 📊 Practitioner Journals & Publications

### 1. **Journal of Portfolio Management** (JPM)
- **Publisher**: Institutional Investor
- **Focus**: Portfolio construction, asset allocation, performance
- **Audience**: Investment managers, CIOs
- **Access**: Subscription (~$500/year) or institutional
- **Why Read**: Bridge academic research and practice
- **Link**: https://jpm.pm-research.com

### 2. **Financial Analysts Journal** (FAJ)
- **Publisher**: CFA Institute
- **Focus**: Investment analysis, portfolio management
- **Audience**: Analysts, PMs, CFAs
- **Access**: FREE for CFA members, $250/year otherwise
- **Link**: https://www.cfainstitute.org/faj
- **Why Read**: High-quality practitioner research

### 3. **Journal of Trading**
- **Focus**: Trading strategies, execution, market microstructure
- **Audience**: Traders, quants
- **Access**: Subscription
- **Why Read**: Practical trading insights

## 🏆 Must-Read Practitioner Papers

### 4. **"The Fundamental Law of Active Management"** (1989)
- **Author**: Richard C. Grinold
- **Journal**: JPM
- **Formula**: IR = IC × √BR (Information Ratio = Skill × Breadth)
- **Why Read**: Foundation of active management
- **Impact**: Still quoted in every PM interview

### 5. **"Facts and Fantasies About Commodity Futures"** (2006)
- **Authors**: Gary Gorton, K. Geert Rouwenhorst
- **Topics**: Commodity risk premium, diversification
- **Why Read**: Evidence for commodity allocation
- **Access**: SSRN

### 6. **"Buffett's Alpha"** (2018)
- **Authors**: Frazzini, Kabiller, Pedersen
- **Journal**: Financial Analysts Journal
- **Why Read**: Quantifies Buffett's strategy (leverage + quality)
- **Impact**: Shows value of consistent strategy
- **Access**: FAJ, SSRN

### 7. **"Trend-Following Risk Premia"** (2012)
- **Authors**: Moskowitz, Ooi, Pedersen
- **Journal**: Journal of Financial Economics
- **Why Read**: Evidence for time-series momentum across assets
- **Impact**: CTA strategy validation

### 8. **"Demystifying Illiquid Assets"** (2019)
- **Authors**: Stafford
- **Journal**: Journal of Portfolio Management
- **Why Read**: How to think about private equity, real estate
- **Insight**: Illiquidity premium may be overstated
