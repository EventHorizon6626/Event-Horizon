# Doctoral / PhD Level - Books

Research-oriented texts and advanced theoretical treatises for academic finance.

---

## 📊 Asset Pricing Theory

### 1. **"Asset Pricing and Portfolio Choice Theory"**
- **Author**: Kerry Back
- **Edition**: 2nd Edition (2017)
- **Publisher**: Oxford University Press
- **Topics**: Continuous-time models, stochastic calculus, consumption-based CAPM
- **Why Read**: PhD-level asset pricing theory
- **Prerequisites**: Stochastic calculus, measure theory
- **Price**: ~$80-100

### 2. **"Financial Decisions and Markets: A Course in Asset Pricing"**
- **Author**: John Y. Campbell
- **Edition**: 2017
- **Publisher**: Princeton University Press
- **Topics**: Intertemporal models, equilibrium asset pricing
- **Why Read**: Campbell's lecture notes from Harvard PhD course
- **Price**: ~$70-90

### 3. **"Empirical Asset Pricing: The Cross Section of Stock Returns"**
- **Author**: Turan G. Bali, Robert F. Engle, Scott Murray
- **Edition**: 2016
- **Topics**: Empirical methods, factor models, anomalies
- **Why Read**: Bridge theory and empirics
- **Price**: ~$80-100

---

## 🧮 Mathematical Finance

### 4. **"Continuous Martingales and Brownian Motion"**
- **Authors**: Daniel Revuz, Marc Yor
- **Edition**: 3rd Edition (1999)
- **Topics**: Stochastic processes, martingale theory
- **Why Read**: Mathematical rigor for continuous-time finance
- **Prerequisites**: Measure theory, real analysis
- **Price**: ~$80-120

### 5. **"Methods of Mathematical Finance"**
- **Authors**: Ioannis Karatzas, Steven E. Shreve
- **Edition**: 1998
- **Topics**: Stochastic calculus, optimal portfolio choice
- **Why Read**: Rigorous mathematical treatment
- **Price**: ~$90-120

### 6. **"Martingale Methods in Financial Modelling"**
- **Author**: Marek Musiela, Marek Rutkowski
- **Edition**: 2nd Edition (2005)
- **Topics**: Interest rate models, exotic options, martingale measures
- **Why Read**: Comprehensive mathematical finance reference
- **Price**: ~$100-150

## 📈 Econometrics & Time Series

### 7. **"Time Series Analysis"**
- **Author**: James D. Hamilton
- **Edition**: 1994 (classic)
- **Topics**: ARIMA, VAR, state space models, Kalman filtering
- **Why Read**: PhD-level econometric theory
- **Price**: ~$100-150

### 8. **"Econometric Analysis of Cross Section and Panel Data"**
- **Author**: Jeffrey M. Wooldridge
- **Edition**: 2nd Edition (2010)
- **Topics**: Panel data, instrumental variables, GMM
- **Why Read**: PhD-level microeconometrics
- **Price**: ~$100-150

### 9. **"Elements of Financial Risk Management"**
- **Author**: Peter F. Christoffersen
- **Edition**: 2nd Edition (2012)
- **Topics**: VaR, GARCH, copulas, backtesting
- **Why Read**: Advanced risk modeling
- **Price**: ~$80-100

## 🏦 Corporate Finance Theory

### 10. **"The Theory of Corporate Finance"**
- **Author**: Jean Tirole
- **Edition**: 2006
- **Topics**: Incomplete contracts, agency theory, governance
- **Why Read**: Nobel Prize winner's comprehensive treatise
- **Price**: ~$90-120

### 11. **"Financial Intermediation and Derivatives Valuation"**
- **Author**: Martino Grasselli, Thorsten Schmidt
- **Edition**: 2022
- **Topics**: Banking theory, counterparty risk
- **Why Read**: Modern treatment of intermediation
- **Price**: ~$80-100

## 📖 Market Microstructure

### 12. **"Market Microstructure Theory"**
- **Authors**: Maureen O'Hara
- **Edition**: 1995
- **Topics**: Adverse selection, inventory models, price discovery
- **Why Read**: Foundation of microstructure theory
- **Price**: ~$80-100

### 13. **"The Microstructure of Financial Markets"**
- **Authors**: Frank de Jong, Barbara Rindi
- **Edition**: 2009
- **Topics**: Limit order books, algorithmic trading
- **Why Read**: Modern microstructure perspectives
- **Price**: ~$70-90

## 🌐 Advanced Topics

### 14. **"Credit Risk: Pricing, Measurement, and Management"**
- **Authors**: Darrell Duffie, Kenneth J. Singleton
- **Edition**: 2003
- **Topics**: Structural models, reduced-form models, CDOs
- **Why Read**: Definitive text on credit risk
- **Price**: ~$80-120

### 15. **"Systemic Risk in Financial Systems"**
- **Editors**: Hurd, Lipton
- **Edition**: 2021
- **Topics**: Network models, contagion, regulation
- **Why Read**: Cutting-edge research on systemic risk
- **Price**: ~$100-150

### 16. **"High-Frequency Trading: A Practical Guide to Algorithmic Strategies**
- **Author**: Irene Aldridge
- **Edition**: 2013
- **Topics**: HFT strategies, latency, market making
- **Why Read**: Academic treatment of HFT
- **Price**: ~$80-120

## 🔬 Research Methods
