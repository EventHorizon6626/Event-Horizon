# Doctoral / PhD Level - Online Resources

Academic seminars, research tools, and professional development for finance PhDs.

---

## 🎓 Online PhD-Level Courses

### 1. **MIT OpenCourseWare - Finance PhD Courses**
- **Courses**: Advanced Corporate Finance, Asset Pricing, Financial Econometrics
- **Instructors**: Professors Wang, Lo, Kogan
- **Cost**: FREE
- **Link**: https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/sloan-school-of-management/
- **Materials**: Lecture notes, problem sets, exams

### 2. **Chicago Booth Finance PhD Course Materials**
- **Available**: Selected course materials online
- **Topics**: Asset pricing, empirical methods
- **Link**: Faculty websites (Cochrane, Fama, Hansen)

### 3. **Stanford GSB Finance Research Seminars**
- **Format**: Recorded seminars on YouTube
- **Link**: Stanford GSB YouTube channel
- **Why Watch**: See cutting-edge research presentations

## 📚 Research Databases & Tools

### 4. **EconLit**
- **Type**: Economic literature database
- **Coverage**: 1.4M+ records from 1969-present
- **Access**: University subscription
- **Link**: https://www.aeaweb.org/econlit/
- **Why Use**: Comprehensive literature search

### 5. **Web of Science / Scopus**
- **Type**: Citation databases
- **Why Use**: Track citations, find related papers
- **Access**: University subscription

### 6. **Google Scholar**
- **Type**: FREE academic search
- **Features**: Citations, alerts, profiles
- **Why Use**: Quick paper lookup, citation tracking

---

## 🛠️ Research Software & Coding

### 7. **Stata**
- **Type**: Statistical software
- **Cost**: $595-$1,595 (student pricing)
- **Link**: https://www.stata.com
- **Why Use**: Standard in empirical finance research
- **Alternative**: R (free), Python (free)

### 8. **MATLAB**
- **Type**: Numerical computing
- **Cost**: FREE for most universities
- **Why Use**: Simulations, numerical methods
- **Link**: https://www.mathworks.com

### 9. **LaTeX (Overleaf)**
- **Type**: Document preparation
- **Cost**: FREE (basic), $15/month (premium)
- **Link**: https://www.overleaf.com
- **Why Use**: Write academic papers

## 📖 Finance Seminars & Conferences (Online)

### 10. **NBER Finance Meetings**
- **Frequency**: Biannual
- **Format**: In-person + some online
- **Link**: https://www.nber.org/conferences
- **Why Attend**: Network, see latest research

### 11. **Virtual Finance Seminar**
- **Format**: Online seminar series
- **Frequency**: Weekly during academic year
- **Link**: https://sites.google.com/view/virtual-finance-seminar
- **Why Attend**: Top researchers present remotely

### 12. **Finance Theory Group (FTG) Meetings**
- **Frequency**: Annual
- **Format**: Invite-only (PhD students can submit)
- **Why Apply**: Theory-focused conference
