# Undergraduate Level - Books

Essential textbooks and foundational readings for building core financial knowledge.

---

## 📊 Financial Markets & Investing

### 1. **"A Random Walk Down Wall Street"** ⭐ START HERE
- **Author**: Burton G. Malkiel
- **Edition**: 13th Edition (2023)
- **Level**: Beginner-Friendly
- **Topics**: Efficient markets, index investing, portfolio theory
- **Why Read**: Classic introduction to investment philosophy, accessible to complete beginners
- **Where to Buy**: Amazon, Barnes & Noble, Audible
- **Price**: ~$15-25 (paperback), $10 (Kindle)
- **Time to Read**: 2-3 weeks

### 2. **"The Intelligent Investor"**
- **Author**: Benjamin Graham (with commentary by Jason Zweig)
- **Edition**: Revised Edition (2006)
- **Level**: Beginner
- **Topics**: Value investing, margin of safety, investor psychology
- **Why Read**: Warren Buffett's #1 recommendation, timeless investing principles
- **Where to Buy**: Amazon, local bookstores
- **Price**: ~$15-30
- **Time to Read**: 3-4 weeks

### 3. **"Common Stocks and Uncommon Profits"**
- **Author**: Philip A. Fisher
- **Edition**: Classic (1958, still relevant)
- **Level**: Beginner-Intermediate
- **Topics**: Growth investing, qualitative analysis, scuttlebutt method
- **Why Read**: Balances Graham's value approach with growth perspective
- **Where to Buy**: Amazon, Wiley
- **Price**: ~$20-25
- **Time to Read**: 2 weeks

### 4. **"Investments"** (Textbook)
- **Authors**: Zvi Bodie, Alex Kane, Alan Marcus
- **Edition**: 13th Edition (2024)
- **Level**: Intermediate (requires some math)
- **Topics**: Asset classes, portfolio theory, derivatives basics, risk management
- **Why Read**: Standard university textbook, comprehensive coverage
- **Where to Buy**: Amazon, campus bookstores, Chegg (rental)
- **Price**: ~$100-200 (new), $50-80 (used/rental)
- **Time to Read**: Full semester (use as reference)

### 5. **"One Up On Wall Street"**
- **Author**: Peter Lynch
- **Edition**: 2000 Edition
- **Level**: Beginner
- **Topics**: Stock picking, retail investor advantages, company analysis
- **Why Read**: Practical advice from legendary fund manager, inspirational
- **Where to Buy**: Amazon
- **Price**: ~$15-20
- **Time to Read**: 2 weeks

## 💼 Corporate Finance

### 6. **"Principles of Corporate Finance"**
- **Authors**: Richard A. Brealey, Stewart C. Myers, Franklin Allen
- **Edition**: 14th Edition (2023)
- **Level**: Intermediate
- **Topics**: NPV, capital budgeting, capital structure, valuation
- **Why Read**: Industry-standard textbook, comprehensive and rigorous
- **Where to Buy**: McGraw-Hill, Amazon, textbook rental sites
- **Price**: ~$150-300 (new), $70-100 (rental)
- **Time to Read**: Full semester

### 7. **"Corporate Finance"**
- **Authors**: Jonathan Berk, Peter DeMarzo
- **Edition**: 5th Edition (2020)
- **Level**: Intermediate
- **Topics**: Same as above, alternative perspective
- **Why Read**: More modern examples, excellent problem sets
- **Where to Buy**: Pearson, Amazon
- **Price**: ~$150-280
- **Time to Read**: Full semester

### 8. **"Valuation: Measuring and Managing the Value of Companies"**
- **Authors**: McKinsey & Company (Tim Koller, Marc Goedhart, David Wessels)
- **Edition**: 7th Edition (2020)
- **Level**: Intermediate-Advanced
- **Topics**: DCF, comparable company analysis, valuation techniques
- **Why Read**: Practitioner-focused, used by investment bankers and consultants
- **Where to Buy**: Wiley, Amazon
- **Price**: ~$60-100
- **Time to Read**: 4-6 weeks

## 🧠 Behavioral Finance & Psychology

- **Level**: Beginner

- **Edition**: 2015
- **Level**: Beginner-Intermediate

- **Why Read**: Entertaining introduction to behavioral finance
- **Where to Buy**: Amazon, W. W. Norton & Company

## 📚 Recommended Reading Order

### Path 1: Practical Investor (3-6 months)
1. "A Random Walk Down Wall Street" - Foundation
2. "The Intelligent Investor" - Philosophy
3. "One Up On Wall Street" - Practice
4. "Financial Statements" (Ittelson) - Skills
5. "Thinking, Fast and Slow" - Psychology

### Path 2: Academic Finance Student (1 year)
1. "Investments" (Bodie, Kane, Marcus) - Core
2. "Principles of Corporate Finance" - Core
3. "Financial Statement Analysis" - Skills
4. "Against the Gods" - Context
5. "Misbehaving" - Behavioral

### Path 3: Aspiring Trader (4-6 months)
1. "Market Wizards" - Inspiration
2. "Technical Analysis" (Murphy) - Skills
3. "Thinking, Fast and Slow" - Psychology
4. "A Random Walk" - Reality check
5. "Manias, Panics, and Crashes" - Risk awareness

## 📊 Budget Guide

**Minimum Budget** (Path 1 - Practical): ~$75-100
**Medium Budget** (Mix of classic + 1-2 textbooks): ~$250-350
**Full Budget** (All resources): ~$800-1,200

**Cost-Saving Tips**:
- Buy used or international editions
- Rent textbooks instead of buying
- Use library resources
- Form study group to share costs
- Look for PDF versions (legally!) through university libraries
