## 🎓 Free Online Courses (MOOCs)

### 1. **Yale - Financial Markets** ⭐ START HERE
- **Instructor**: Robert Shiller (Nobel Prize winner)
- **Platform**: Coursera / Yale OpenCourseWare
- **Duration**: 7 weeks (~7-14 hours/week)
- **Level**: Beginner
- **Topics**: Stocks, bonds, derivatives, behavioral finance, regulation
- **Cost**: FREE (audit) or $49 for certificate
- **Link**: https://www.coursera.org/learn/financial-markets-global
- **Why Take**: World-class professor, comprehensive overview

### 2. **MIT - Introduction to Financial and Managerial Accounting**
- **Platform**: MIT OpenCourseWare
- **Duration**: Self-paced
- **Level**: Beginner
- **Topics**: Financial statements, accounting principles
- **Cost**: FREE
- **Link**: https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-501-introduction-to-financial-and-managerial-accounting-spring-2004/
- **Why Take**: Learn to read financial statements

### 3. **Khan Academy - Finance and Capital Markets**
- **Platform**: Khan Academy
- **Duration**: Self-paced (~40 hours total)
- **Level**: Beginner
- **Topics**: Interest, valuation, stocks, bonds, options, corporate finance
- **Cost**: FREE
- **Link**: https://www.khanacademy.org/economics-finance-domain/core-finance
- **Why Take**: Excellent for complete beginners, visual explanations

### 4. **Coursera - Investment Management Specialization (University of Geneva)**
- **Platform**: Coursera
- **Duration**: 4 courses (~3-4 months)
- **Level**: Beginner-Intermediate
- **Topics**: Portfolio management, asset allocation, risk management
- **Cost**: FREE (audit) or $49/month for certificate
- **Link**: https://www.coursera.org/specializations/investment-management
- **Why Take**: Structured learning path, industry-recognized

### 5. **University of Michigan - Principles of Valuation**
- **Platform**: Coursera
- **Duration**: 4 weeks
- **Level**: Intermediate
- **Topics**: Time value of money, bond/stock valuation, NPV
- **Cost**: FREE (audit)
- **Link**: https://www.coursera.org/learn/valuationprinciple
- **Why Take**: Foundation for corporate finance

## 🎯 Recommended Learning Path

### Month 1-2: Foundation
1. Khan Academy Finance course (FREE)
2. Yale Financial Markets (Coursera)
3. Start following Yahoo Finance daily
4. Watch The Plain Bagel YouTube series

### Month 3-4: Practice
1. Open Investopedia simulator account
2. Practice paper trading
3. Read Buffett's annual letters
4. Subscribe to Abnormal Returns blog

### Month 5-6: Specialize
1. Choose: Value, Growth, Technical, or Quant focus
2. Take relevant Udemy course
3. Start backtesting strategies on Portfolio Visualizer
4. Read specialized blogs in your area

### Ongoing:
- Listen to 1 podcast episode/week
- Read 2-3 articles/week (Abnormal Returns)
- Paper trade regularly
- Track real portfolio if ready
