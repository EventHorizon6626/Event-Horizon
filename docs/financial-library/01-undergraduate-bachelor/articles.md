# Undergraduate Level - Articles & Papers

Foundational academic papers and accessible articles for building financial literacy.

---

## 📄 Classic Foundational Papers (Must-Read)

### 1. **"Portfolio Selection"** (1952) 🏆
- **Author**: Harry Markowitz
- **Journal**: The Journal of Finance, Vol. 7, No. 1
- **Topics**: Modern Portfolio Theory, mean-variance optimization
- **Why Read**: Nobel Prize-winning paper that founded modern portfolio theory
- **Difficulty**: Moderate (some math required)
- **Length**: 14 pages
- **Where to Access**:
  - JSTOR: https://www.jstor.org/stable/2975974
  - Free PDF: Search Google Scholar
- **Key Takeaway**: Diversification reduces risk; investors should consider portfolio-level risk, not individual stock risk

### 2. **"The Efficient Market Hypothesis: A Review"** (1970)
- **Author**: Eugene F. Fama
- **Journal**: The Journal of Finance
- **Topics**: Market efficiency, random walk, information incorporation
- **Why Read**: Defines three forms of market efficiency still debated today
- **Difficulty**: Moderate
- **Length**: ~30 pages
- **Where to Access**: JSTOR, Google Scholar
- **Key Takeaway**: Prices reflect all available information (in efficient markets)

### 3. **"The Capital Asset Pricing Model: Theory and Evidence"** (2004)
- **Authors**: Eugene F. Fama, Kenneth R. French
- **Journal**: Journal of Economic Perspectives
- **Topics**: CAPM, beta, risk-return relationship
- **Why Read**: Critical review of CAPM, accessible explanation
- **Difficulty**: Moderate
- **Length**: 25 pages
- **Where to Access**:
  - Free PDF: https://pubs.aeaweb.org/doi/pdfplus/10.1257/0895330042162430
- **Key Takeaway**: CAPM is elegant but empirically limited

### 4. **"Does the Stock Market Overreact?"** (1985)
- **Authors**: Werner F. M. De Bondt, Richard Thaler
- **Journal**: The Journal of Finance
- **Topics**: Behavioral finance, mean reversion, investor psychology
- **Why Read**: Early behavioral finance paper showing market inefficiencies
- **Difficulty**: Easy-Moderate
- **Length**: 27 pages
- **Where to Access**: JSTOR, Google Scholar
- **Key Takeaway**: Markets overreact to news, creating contrarian opportunities

## 💡 Behavioral Finance & Psychology

### 5. **"Prospect Theory: An Analysis of analyzer under Risk"** (1979)
- **Authors**: Daniel Kahneman, Amos Tversky
- **Journal**: Econometrica
- **Topics**: Loss aversion, framing effects, risk preferences
- **Why Read**: Foundation of behavioral economics (Nobel Prize work)
- **Difficulty**: Moderate-Hard (some technical sections)
- **Length**: 33 pages
- **Where to Access**: JSTOR, Google Scholar
- **Key Takeaway**: People are loss-averse and don't behave rationally

### 6. **"Investor Sentiment and the Cross-Section of Stock Returns"** (2006)
- **Authors**: Malcolm Baker, Jeffrey Wurgler
- **Journal**: The Journal of Finance
- **Topics**: Sentiment indicators, market psychology
- **Why Read**: Shows how investor mood affects prices
- **Difficulty**: Moderate
- **Length**: 30 pages
- **Where to Access**: JSTOR, SSRN
- **Key Takeaway**: Hard-to-value stocks are most affected by sentiment

## 📊 Value & Fundamental Investing

### 7. **"The Cross-Section of Expected Stock Returns"** (1992)
- **Authors**: Eugene F. Fama, Kenneth R. French
- **Journal**: The Journal of Finance
- **Topics**: Size factor, value factor, factor investing
- **Why Read**: Introduces the famous Fama-French 3-factor model
- **Difficulty**: Moderate-Hard
- **Length**: 40 pages
- **Where to Access**: JSTOR, Google Scholar
- **Key Takeaway**: Size and value factors explain returns better than beta alone

### 8. **"Contrarian Investment, Extrapolation, and Risk"** (1994)
- **Authors**: Josef Lakonishok, Andrei Shleifer, Robert Vishny
- **Journal**: The Journal of Finance
- **Topics**: Value investing, growth vs value
- **Why Read**: Empirical evidence for value investing
- **Difficulty**: Moderate
- **Length**: 35 pages
- **Where to Access**: JSTOR
- **Key Takeaway**: Value stocks outperform growth stocks

## 📈 Market Anomalies & Patterns

### 9. **"Returns to Buying Winners and Selling Losers: Implications for Stock Market Efficiency"** (1993)
- **Authors**: Narasimhan Jegadeesh, Sheridan Titman
- **Journal**: The Journal of Finance
- **Topics**: Momentum investing, market inefficiency
- **Why Read**: Documents the momentum effect in stocks
- **Difficulty**: Moderate
- **Length**: 25 pages
- **Where to Access**: JSTOR, Google Scholar
- **Key Takeaway**: Past winners continue to outperform (for 3-12 months)

### 10. **"A Five-Factor Asset Pricing Model"** (2015)
- **Authors**: Eugene F. Fama, Kenneth R. French
- **Journal**: Journal of Financial Economics
- **Topics**: Factor investing, profitability, investment factors
- **Why Read**: Latest evolution of factor models
- **Difficulty**: Moderate-Hard
- **Length**: 30 pages
- **Where to Access**: ScienceDirect, SSRN
- **Key Takeaway**: Adding profitability and investment factors improves explanatory power

## 💼 Corporate Finance Foundations

### 11. **"The Cost of Capital, Corporation Finance and the Theory of Investment"** (1958)
- **Authors**: Franco Modigliani, Merton H. Miller
- **Journal**: The American Economic Review
- **Topics**: Capital structure, MM theorem
- **Why Read**: Nobel Prize-winning work on corporate finance theory
- **Difficulty**: Hard (technical)
- **Length**: 30 pages
- **Where to Access**: JSTOR, Google Scholar
- **Key Takeaway**: In perfect markets, capital structure doesn't affect firm value

### 12. **"Agency Problems and the Theory of the Firm"** (1976)
- **Authors**: Eugene F. Fama, Michael C. Jensen
- **Journal**: Journal of Political Economy
- **Topics**: Principal-agent problem, corporate governance
- **Why Read**: Explains conflicts between managers and shareholders
- **Difficulty**: Moderate
- **Length**: 20 pages
- **Where to Access**: JSTOR, Google Scholar
- **Key Takeaway**: Incentive alignment is crucial in corporate governance

## 🔍 Accessible Review Articles

### 13. **"The Equity Premium: A Puzzle"** (1985)
- **Authors**: Rajnish Mehra, Edward C. Prescott
- **Journal**: Journal of Monetary Economics
- **Topics**: Equity risk premium, asset pricing
- **Why Read**: Identifies one of finance's biggest unsolved mysteries
- **Difficulty**: Moderate-Hard
- **Length**: 15 pages
- **Where to Access**: ScienceDirect, Google Scholar
- **Key Takeaway**: Stocks have historically outperformed bonds by more than theory predicts

### 14. **"Anomalies: The January Effect"** (1987)
- **Author**: Jay R. Ritter
- **Journal**: Journal of Economic Perspectives
- **Topics**: Calendar effects, market anomalies
- **Why Read**: Easy-to-read explanation of seasonal patterns
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Length**: 12 pages
- **Where to Access**: AEA website (free)
- **Key Takeaway**: Small-cap stocks tend to outperform in January

## 🌐 Online-Only Articles (Free Access)

### 15. **"Common Risk Factors in the Returns on Stocks and Bonds"** (1993)
- **Authors**: Eugene F. Fama, Kenneth R. French
- **Journal**: Journal of Financial Economics
- **Topics**: Factor investing, bond returns
- **Why Read**: Extends factor model to bonds
- **Difficulty**: Moderate
- **Where to Access**: ScienceDirect, SSRN
- **Key Takeaway**: Similar factors drive stock and bond returns

### 16. **"The Disposition Effect in Securities Trading: An Experimental Analysis"** (1998)
- **Authors**: Terrance Odean
- **Journal**: The Journal of Finance
- **Topics**: Behavioral biases, trading psychology
- **Why Read**: Shows investors hold losers too long and sell winners too soon
- **Difficulty**: Easy-Moderate
- **Length**: 25 pages
- **Where to Access**: JSTOR, Google Scholar
- **Key Takeaway**: Loss aversion causes poor selling decisions

## 🔍 How to Access Papers

### Free Access:
1. **Google Scholar** - Search paper title, often find free PDF
2. **Author Websites** - Many professors post their papers
3. **SSRN** - Pre-prints and working papers
4. **University Library** - If you're a student
5. **ResearchGate** - Request from authors

### Paid Access:
- **JSTOR** - $19.50/month for 10 papers
- **Elsevier** - Individual article purchase (~$30-40 each)
- **Institution Subscriptions** - Free through university/employer

### Tips:
- Search "[Paper Title] PDF" in Google
- Check if your library has alumni access
- Email authors directly (often happy to share)
- Use Sci-Hub (legal grey area)

## 📊 Difficulty Guide

**Easy** (🟢): Minimal math, readable prose, practical insights
**Moderate** (🟡): Some quantitative analysis, basic statistics knowledge helpful
**Hard** (🔴): Technical, requires econometrics/calculus background
